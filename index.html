Frequently asked questions

You've got questions. We've got answers.

Here are the questions we hear most. Still have more? Contact us.

Are your estimates really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the trees with you and give you a written price. No charge and no pressure.

Do you clean up and haul everything away?

Every job ends with a full clean-up. Branches, logs and debris are hauled off and the area is raked and blown clean, unless you'd like to keep the firewood or chips.

Will you protect my roof, fence and lawn?

Yes. Large limbs are lowered in sections with ropes instead of dropped, and every cut is planned around your house, fences, beds and driveway.

Can you grind the stump after removing a tree?

Yes. We can grind it the same day as the removal or schedule it separately. The stump is ground below grade so you can replant or lay sod.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?

Some DFW cities regulate removing large or protected trees. We'll let you know if your city requires a permit before any work starts.

How often should my trees be trimmed?

Most yard trees do well with a trim every few years, and sooner if limbs are touching the roof, blocking light or hanging over the driveway. We'll tell you honestly during the estimate.

Do you handle storm damage?

Yes. We clear fallen trees and broken or hanging limbs, then chip and haul the debris away. Call or text (817) 776-0732 and send photos so we come prepared.

What is tree cabling?

Cabling adds support to heavy or split limbs so they are less likely to break in high wind. We will tell you during the estimate if a tree is a good candidate.

What areas do you serve?

We provide service to all of Dallas–Fort Worth and the surrounding cities. Not sure we cover your address? Call or text and we'll tell you right away.
